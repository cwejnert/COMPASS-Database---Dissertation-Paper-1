- At World, all eight displayed pooled cells now point toward High-RE: four outcomes at 1.5C and four at 2C. DLE gap and deprived headcount are two readings of the same deprivation distribution, so this is six of six distinct outcome-by-ambition findings rather than eight independent confirmations.

The final evidence judgments are:

1. **Employment:** direction supported; pooled magnitude is strongly model-composition sensitive.
2. **DLE gap and deprived headcount:** database associations, not model-independent High-RE effects. Both are driven by the same final-energy distribution mechanism and by REMIND's one-sided representation.
3. **PM2.5 mortality:** a small, coverage-qualified and composition-sensitive association. The most comparable model-project-ambition cells lean High-RE, but the pooled direction can reverse under influential-family exclusions.
4. **SCI-vetted:** not an independent cross-model validation set in this classification. At World, only one family contains both arms for each outcome, so its pooled contrasts remain composition comparisons.

## What the three tiers do

### Tier 1: quantitative screen

For every database, ambition view, region, and outcome, Tier 1 reports:

- scenario-weighted High-CDR and High-RE medians;
- within-family contrasts wherever a family contains at least one scenario in each arm;
- an equal-family mean so scenario-rich families do not automatically dominate;
- leave-one-family-out estimates to identify influential families;
- arm counts, comparable-family counts, and robust-family counts.

A family or matched cell is called robust only when both arms have at least three scenarios. A 20-percentage-point gap between the pooled and equal-family estimates flags magnitude sensitivity. These are transparent screening rules, not statistical significance tests.

### Tier 2: matched project/mechanism audit

Tier 2 compares High-CDR and High-RE only inside the same model version, project, and ambition level. This is the strongest available observational comparison because it removes three major sources of composition variation. It does not create a comparison where the database contains only one arm.

Tier 2 then checks the outcome mechanism:

- employment: capacity additions and installed capacity multiplied by technology- and region-specific job factors;
- deprivation: scenario final energy plus the regional Gini define a lognormal distribution; the threshold determines the deprived share and energy gap;
- mortality: reported precursor emissions are passed through RFASST, spatial allocation, and the PM2.5 concentration-response calculation.

### Tier 3: targeted scenario audit

Tier 3 is triggered only by sole or two-scenario family-arm comparators, sparse matched project cells, or dominant project clusters. It is not a line-by-line audit of every scenario. The purpose is to explain cases that can change interpretation, not to manufacture precision from sparse cells.

## Full versus SCI-vetted

SCI-vetted has 69 High-CDR and 69 High-RE scenarios with ambitions pooled, 6/6 at 1.5C, and 63/63 at 2C. Equal arm sizes do not imply equal model composition.

At World, only one SCI-vetted family contains both arms for each outcome. The other families are largely one-sided. As a result:

- SCI pooled employment points High-RE, but cannot establish cross-model agreement.
- SCI pooled DLE and headcount reverse at 2C, while the only informative family points High-RE.
- SCI pooled mortality reverses with ambitions pooled and at 2C, while IMAGE, the only informative family, points High-RE.

SCI-vetted therefore answers whether a screening subset changes the pooled database description; it does not independently validate the pathway contrast after accounting for model composition.

### DLE gap and deprived headcount

REMIND supplies 220 of 301 Full High-RE employment-complete scenarios with ambitions pooled, but only one High-CDR comparator; for DLE/headcount the corresponding Full counts are 220 High-RE and one High-CDR. Removing REMIND reverses both deprivation pooled directions.

The mechanism is expected from the method. Within the larger model families, World final energy per person is strongly negatively associated with the DLE gap and deprived share. REMIND's High-RE project clusters generally have higher final energy than its sole High-CDR comparator. The lognormal DLE calculation therefore produces smaller gaps and headcounts for those selected pathways.

The robust matched cells are mixed:

- DLE gap: one of four robust cells favors High-RE; three favor High-CDR.
- Deprived headcount: two of four robust cells favor High-RE; two favor High-CDR.

The two deprivation measures should be presented as one substantive finding measured two ways. They move together because both are calculated from the same fitted distribution and threshold.

## Synthesis rule for the paper

Do not validate the pooled result by asking whether a simple majority of families agrees. Families differ in arm coverage, scenario counts, project structure, and relevance to each outcome.

Report three estimands side by side:

1. **Scenario-weighted pooled:** describes the database as sampled.
2. **Equal-family:** shows whether scenario-rich families determine the average family direction.
3. **Matched model-project-ambition:** provides the strongest available within-design comparison, only where both arms exist.

Then apply the evidence labels:

- **Cross-model supported:** multiple robust comparable families/projects agree, pooled and equal-family directions agree, and no influential exclusion flips direction.
- **Direction supported; magnitude composition-sensitive:** direction survives, but pooled size depends materially on family composition.
- **Database association only:** the pooled direction reverses when a dominant family is removed or robust matched cells disagree.
- **Not identified across model families:** fewer than two families contain both arms.
- **Coverage-qualified:** the outcome has a documented reporting ceiling; conclusions apply to the reporting-complete sample.

The current final labels are employment = direction supported/magnitude sensitive; deprivation = database association only; mortality = coverage-qualified/composition-sensitive, with matched evidence leaning High-RE.
